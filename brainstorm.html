What should we test?

Pick exercise (heart rate goes up) or the opposite (calming down and meditation). Your experiment needs two conditions to compare, because that is what makes the p-value work later. Steal an idea below or invent your own.

Ground rules for every experiment

  • No caffeine, energy drinks, or supplements this year.
  • Light to moderate exercise only. Stop right away if you feel dizzy, sick, or hurt.
  • Your teacher must approve your plan before you collect any data.
  • Keep health information private. Use first names or initials in anything you share.
