Check the AI. AI can make math mistakes. In Google Sheets, type =T.TEST(A2:A6, B2:B6, 2, 1) with your own cell ranges. The last number is 1 for paired data and 3 for separate groups. Your answers should match.

What a p-value means. It is the chance of seeing a difference this big if the condition really did nothing. A p-value below 0.05 means the difference is probably real (statistically significant). Above 0.05 means you cannot be sure it was not luck.
