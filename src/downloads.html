Can't open a PowerPoint (.pptx) file?

Use Google Slides. It works with your school Google account.

  1. Download the PowerPoint file.
  2. Go to drive.google.com and click New, then File upload. Pick the file.
  3. Right-click the file in Drive. Choose Open with, then Google Slides.
  4. Click File, then Save as Google Slides. Now it is your own Google Slides copy.
