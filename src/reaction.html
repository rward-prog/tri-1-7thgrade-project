Reaction time test

Click as fast as you can when the box turns green. The test shows your time in milliseconds (ms). Use this to measure reaction time for your experiment.

Keep it fair: same every trial

  • Use the same device, the same hand, and the same room every time. Touch screens usually give faster times than a mouse, so do not switch.
  • Do 1 practice round first. It does not count.
  • Do 5 real tries. The average is one data point for your experiment.
  • If you click too early, that try does not count. Wait for green.
